    FAQ

    Does dying always mean a loss?

    No. RaidReceipt evaluates the values you enter. A death can still be profitable or earn a non-F grade with meaningful combat or support stats.

    Does it use live ABI prices?

    No. This MVP uses your estimates and does not claim exact game-economy data.

    Is RaidReceipt official?

    No. RaidReceipt is an unofficial community tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by MoreFun Studios or Arena Breakout: Infinite.
