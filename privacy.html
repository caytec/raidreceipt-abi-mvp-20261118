Simple by design

What we collect

The calculator works locally in your browser. We do not require accounts and do not intentionally collect player names, event notes, or raid forms.

Optional AI

If enabled by the deployment owner, the server may send structured raid values to the configured AI provider to create optional copy. API keys remain server-side. Do not enter personal or confidential information.

Share URLs

Share links encode selected raid values in the URL. Anyone with a shared URL can read those values. Avoid sharing anything you consider sensitive.
